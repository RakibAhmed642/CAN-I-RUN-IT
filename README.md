## 🚀 What is CAN I RUN IT?

**CAN I RUN IT (CIRI)** is a free web-based tool that instantly answers the question every PC gamer types into Google before buying or downloading a game:

> **"Can I run it?" · "Can you run it?" · "Can my PC run it?" · "Will it run on my PC?"**

Enter your PC specs — or let the built-in **AI hardware scanner** auto-detect them — pick any game, and CIRI instantly tells you whether your setup meets the **minimum** or **recommended system requirements**, along with a realistic **FPS estimate** at **1080p, 1440p, and 4K**.

No downloads, no installs, no sign-up walls, no sketchy pop-ups — just a clean, fast, and honest **PC game compatibility checker** built for gamers who want a real answer, not a guess.

🔗 **Try it now:** [https://canirunit.site](https://canirunit.site)

## 💡 Why CIRI Exists

Most "can you run it" tools online are outdated, cluttered with ads, or rely on stale hardware databases that don't cover the latest GPUs, CPUs, or game releases. CIRI was built to fix that:

- ✅ Up-to-date system requirements for **new and trending releases** (Battlefield 6, Arc Raiders, Crimson Desert, Borderlands 4, Forza Horizon 6, and more)
- ✅ Real **FPS benchmark data**, not just a pass/fail checkmark
- ✅ A modern, ad-light interface — fast on both desktop and mobile
- ✅ An **AI-powered scanner** that reads your hardware automatically, so you don't need to know your GPU model by heart

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I check "can I run it" for a specific game?**
A: Go to [canirunit.site](https://canirunit.site), enter or scan your PC specs, search for the game, and CIRI instantly compares it to that game's minimum and recommended requirements.

**Q: Does CIRI work for laptops?**
A: Yes — CIRI supports both desktop PCs and gaming laptops, including systems with integrated graphics.

**Q: Is the AI hardware scanner safe to use?**
A: The scanner only reads local hardware information needed to compare against game requirements — it doesn't require downloads or installs.

**Q: Can I check "can you run it" without knowing my GPU or CPU model?**
A: Yes — that's exactly what the AI hardware scanner is for. It detects your specs automatically so you don't need to look anything up manually.

**Q: Does CIRI show FPS estimates or just minimum/recommended specs?**
A: Both. CIRI gives you a spec-comparison verdict **and** a real-world FPS benchmark estimate at 1080p, 1440p, and 4K.

**Q: Is CAN I RUN IT free?**
A: Yes, 100% free to use, with no account or sign-up required.

**Q: How often is the game requirements database updated?**
A: New and trending titles are added regularly, including day-one coverage for major releases.
